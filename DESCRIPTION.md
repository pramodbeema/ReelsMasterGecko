# Reels Master for Firefox

Take full control of YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and Facebook Reels on Firefox with essential desktop superpowers!

Short-form videos on YouTube, Instagram, and Facebook are designed for mobile swiping, leaving desktop browser users without basic controls like spacebar play/pause, playback speed, timeline scrubbing, seeking, and hands-free watching.

Reels Master brings a full suite of desktop keyboard shortcuts, customizable seeking, speed controls, hands-free auto-scrolling, an interactive Instagram timeline scrubber, and on-screen controls directly to YouTube, Instagram, and Facebook — seamlessly integrated into modern dark interfaces.

---

### ✨ Key Features

• ⏯️ Space Bar Play/Pause (All 3 Platforms):
Press Space (or K) to pause and play without unwanted page scrolling.

• 🔁 Hands-Free Auto-Scroll:
Click the on-screen "Auto scroll" button (on the left of Reels on Instagram & Facebook, or in the action sidebar on YouTube Shorts). When the video finishes, it automatically advances to the next video with smart loop detection.

• ⏱️ Interactive Instagram Timeline Scrubber:
Hover your mouse over any Instagram Reel to reveal an interactive time bar with a live progress indicator and timestamp. Click or drag to seek using your mouse.

• ⚡ Playback Speed Controls:
Speed up or slow down any Short or Reel with centered on-screen HUD speed feedback:
- > (or Shift + .) — Speed up (1.25x, 1.5x, 1.75x, 2.0x)
- < (or Shift + ,) — Slow down (0.75x, 0.5x, 0.25x)

• ⏩ Customizable Seeking:
Skip intro fluff or replay moments with arrow keys (← / →). Choose your preferred jump duration from the popup (2s, 5s, 10s, 15s or 1-30s slider).

• ⏮️ Instant Restart from 0:00:
Press 0 to immediately restart the active video from the beginning.

• 🔇 Volume & Mute Shortcuts:
- M — Instant mute / unmute toggle
- Shift + ↑ / Shift + ↓ — Adjust volume in 10% increments with HUD display

• 🎧 Audio Track Navigator (YouTube Shorts):
Press A to open the audio track selection menu with full keyboard navigation.

• 💬 Captions Toggle:
Press C to toggle closed captions and subtitles.

• 🔄 Convert to Regular Video:
Convert Shorts or Reels to regular video player with Ctrl + Shift + F (Mac: Cmd + Shift + F) or the on-screen Convert button.

---

### ⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts Reference

- Space / K: Play / Pause video (no page scroll)
- > / < (Shift + . / ,): Playback speed (0.25x – 2.0x)
- ← / →: Seek backward / forward
- Hover & Drag: Interactive timeline scrubber (Instagram)
- 0: Restart from 0:00
- M: Mute / Unmute
- Shift + ↑ / ↓: Volume Up / Down
- C: Captions Toggle
- A: Audio Track Menu (YouTube)
- Ctrl + Shift + F: Convert to standard video format
